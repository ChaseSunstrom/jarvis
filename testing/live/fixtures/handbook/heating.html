Heating and hot water

The boiler in this fixture house is a Vesper 24kW combination boiler, installed in 2019 and serviced every March.

The maximum safe boiler pressure is 2.5 bar. Normal running pressure is between 1.0 and 1.5 bar when cold. Below 0.8 bar the boiler locks out and must be repressurised with the filling loop under the unit.

The recommended flow temperature for the radiators is 55 °C, which is lower than the factory default of 75 °C and cuts gas use by roughly 12 % in this house.

The hot water cylinder holds 210 litres and is reheated between 04:30 and 05:30 daily.
